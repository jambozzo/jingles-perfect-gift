A story about finding God's purpose for our gifts.

Jingle McJangle is no ordinary elf — he's a master woodcarver with dreams as big as the North Pole itself. From wooden ducks to race cars to bird calls, Jingle dreams of sharing his gifts with children everywhere. But when he finally gets his chance to prove himself in Santa's workshop, he runs into one eensy weensy problem: wooden toys are outdated!

Now Jingle must find a new purpose — and it's a lot harder than it looks. Between decorating disasters, cookie catastrophes, and reindeer-flying fiascos, he starts to wonder if his talent belongs anywhere at all. But everything begins to change on the night of Christmas Eve…

Join Jingle as he learns of God's true purpose for his gift — one that turns out to be more wonderful than he could have ever imagined.

Told entirely in rhyme across 44 full-color pages, it's an easy, musical read-aloud for the Advent and Christmas season — the kind of book a family returns to every year. The large landscape hardcover is sized for lap-reading and for lingering over the art.
